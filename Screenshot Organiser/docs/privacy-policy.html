Privacy Policy — Screenshot Organiser

Last updated: 2026

Screenshot Organiser ("the app") helps you organise screenshots on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The app runs entirely on your device. No data ever leaves it.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

Permissions Used and Why

Permission Purpose
Read media images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) Detect newly captured screenshots.
All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Move screenshots into folders you choose anywhere on device storage.
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) Show the quick "move screenshot" popup after a screenshot is taken.
Foreground service / notifications Keep the screenshot monitor running and show its status.

All file operations happen locally and only on files and folders you select.

Data Retention & Deletion

The app stores only a small local preference (your chosen default folder). Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Children

The app does not target children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published at this URL and reflected in the app listing.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub or contact the developer via the Play Store listing.
